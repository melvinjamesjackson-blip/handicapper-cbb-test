How PASS / LEAN works

Provisional screening rule: absolute model-vs-market difference at least 3 points for spread or 5 points for total produces a LEAN. Otherwise it displays PASS. These thresholds are interface heuristics, not backtested betting edges. A missing projection or market line always produces PASS. No win probability, EV, or confidence claim is fabricated.

Game-Day 1.0 is based on prior-season opponent-aware scoring; not possession-calibrated. Current roster, transfer, injury and market context are not incorporated. Verify team venue and prices before use.
